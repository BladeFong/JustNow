JustNow is a family-friendly task manager that turns task completion into a blooming garden.

Children complete tasks, take a photo of their achievement, and each photo becomes petals that light up flowers throughout the week. Collect 5 flowers in a week for a surprise reward.

Features:
- Quadrant-based task organization (Urgent/Important, popularized by Stephen Covey)
- Photo achievement wall — every completed task can be recorded with a photo
- Weekly flower reward system — petals build flowers, flowers unlock surprises
- Multi-user support for family tablets — each child has their own garden
- Schedule profiles for students, teachers, and professionals
- Holiday-aware time periods (summer/winter vacation, spring festival)
- Time period customization with linked start/end times
- Tag management and powerful search with jieba tokenization
- Adaptive layout for phones, tablets, landscape and portrait
- 100% offline — photos and data stay on your device, no account needed
